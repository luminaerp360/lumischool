Kenya CBC Teacher Roles

Class Teacher

Responsible for pastoral care, attendance, and overall welfare of one specific class or stream.

Subject Teacher

Teaches one or more subjects across multiple classes. Handles CATs (30%), Mid-Terms, and End-Term (70%) exams.

HOD (Head of Department)

Senior teacher overseeing a subject department (e.g. Languages, Sciences, Humanities). Coordinates exams and curriculum.

Board of Management (BOM)

BOM Composition

Chairperson (community/religious), parent representatives, teachers' rep, non-teaching staff rep, and government/TSC rep.

BOM Mandate

Responsible for school governance, hiring support staff, managing school finances, and infrastructure under Ministry of Education guidelines.
